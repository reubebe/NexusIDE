Studio

Edit local images, assemble video clips and create page layouts. Every export preserves its sources and records its recipe.

FFmpeg is required for image and video edits. Engine availability is checked on request.

Source

Export preview

Image and raster photography adjustments

PNG, JPEG and WebP. Export is an 8-bit PNG. RAW development, layers and color-profile fidelity are not supported by this engine.

Video timeline

Edit the ordered clips and source in/out seconds. A new MP4 sequence is rendered with audio; source files are retained. Maximum 24 clips / 10 minutes.

Design a page

Edit text and rectangles on a fixed-size page. Export is SVG with an editable recipe; fonts use the viewer's sans-serif font.

Motion keyframes

Animate the selected raster image between two positions on a 1280×720 canvas. Linear motion, 24 fps, maximum 30 seconds.

PDF pages and comments

macOS only; requires installed Swift tools and Apple PDFKit. Select, reorder, rotate and comment on pages into a new PDF. Original signatures may be invalidated in the derivative. This does not perform redaction, OCR or signature verification.

      Task and team budgets
      Limits apply across a team's stages. Token limits are checked after each response and can overshoot by one response per in-flight request. Unknown usage stops a token-budgeted run. Nexus does not track remaining subscription quota; check the official CLI.

Stop the current terminal before switching accounts. These CLIs use their own permissions and can change project files.
